    About

    I design experiences that feel human — starting from empathy, not assumptions.

    As a psychologist and UX researcher, I research mental models, decision-making psychology, and the moments when trust is built or broken. I've studied accessibility barriers in fintech, user trust in AI systems, and behavior change in health apps — every project pairs rigorous methodology with genuine user empathy, aimed at experiences that actually work for people.

    My focus: technology that respects user autonomy, builds real trust, and delivers genuine value.
